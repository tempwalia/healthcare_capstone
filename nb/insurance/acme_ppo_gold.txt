TITLE: Acme PPO Gold
CATEGORY: Insurance Plan
POLICY NUMBER FORMAT: ACME-991123
PLAN TYPE: PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)

COVERAGE SUMMARY
Acme PPO Gold is Acme's higher-tier plan, with the broadest in-network specialist access of the
plans on this platform. Referrals verified against this plan connect the patient with three
in-network doctors on the platform's provider directory today, spanning orthopedics and related
specialties.

COST TO THE PATIENT
Estimated copay: $40 per specialist visit under this plan.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Like every plan on this platform, referrals involving spine-related diagnosis codes (ICD-10 M51 or
M54 — see `prior_authorization_policy.txt`) require prior authorization under this plan. This does
not block the referral; it's a flag for the care team that formal authorization paperwork may be
needed alongside the visit.

WHEN VERIFICATION FAILS
If a policy number doesn't match this plan's expected format, or the plan can't be recognized at
all, eligibility verification returns as not verified and the referral pauses for a care
coordinator to review — this is the same handling every plan on the platform gets, not specific to
Acme.

HOW TO COMPARE THIS PLAN
Acme PPO Gold has a higher copay than Acme HMO Silver ($40 vs $25) but noticeably broader network
access (3 in-network doctors vs 2). Compared to Horizon Blue PPO, it covers a different, mostly
non-overlapping set of in-network doctors — which plan is "better" for a specific referral depends
on which doctor the patient actually needs to see.
