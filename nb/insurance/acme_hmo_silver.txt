TITLE: Acme HMO Silver
CATEGORY: Insurance Plan
POLICY NUMBER FORMAT: ACME-778890
PLAN TYPE: HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)

COVERAGE SUMMARY
Acme HMO Silver is Acme's lower-cost, narrower-network plan on this platform, with 2 in-network
doctors in the provider directory today.

COST TO THE PATIENT
Estimated copay: $25 per specialist visit under this plan — the lowest copay of the four plans on
this platform.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Like every plan on this platform, referrals involving spine-related diagnosis codes (ICD-10 M51 or
M54 — see `prior_authorization_policy.txt`) require prior authorization under this plan.

WHEN VERIFICATION FAILS
If a policy number doesn't match this plan's expected format, or the plan can't be recognized at
all, eligibility verification returns as not verified and the referral pauses for a care
coordinator to review.

HOW TO COMPARE THIS PLAN
Acme HMO Silver has the lowest copay of the four plans on this platform, but the narrowest network
alongside Horizon Blue PPO (2 in-network doctors each). It shares one in-network doctor with Acme
PPO Gold and one with UnitedCare Basic HMO — a patient choosing between HMO Silver and PPO Gold is
mainly trading a lower copay for a smaller specialist pool.
