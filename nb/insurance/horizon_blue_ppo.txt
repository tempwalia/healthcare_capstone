TITLE: Horizon Blue PPO
CATEGORY: Insurance Plan
POLICY NUMBER FORMAT: HORIZON-556677
PLAN TYPE: PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)

COVERAGE SUMMARY
Horizon Blue PPO covers a distinct pair of in-network doctors on this platform's provider
directory, separate from Acme's network — a patient on this plan should expect a different set of
in-network specialist options than an Acme plan member.

COST TO THE PATIENT
Estimated copay: $30 per specialist visit under this plan — between Acme HMO Silver's $25 and Acme
PPO Gold's $40.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Like every plan on this platform, referrals involving spine-related diagnosis codes (ICD-10 M51 or
M54 — see `prior_authorization_policy.txt`) require prior authorization under this plan.

WHEN VERIFICATION FAILS
If a policy number doesn't match this plan's expected format, or the plan can't be recognized at
all, eligibility verification returns as not verified and the referral pauses for a care
coordinator to review.

HOW TO COMPARE THIS PLAN
Horizon Blue PPO's copay ($30) sits between the two Acme plans, but its in-network doctor list does
not overlap with Acme PPO Gold's at all — a patient who specifically needs to see a doctor in
Acme's network won't get in-network coverage from Horizon Blue PPO, regardless of copay. Compare
against the specific doctor's network status, not copay alone.
