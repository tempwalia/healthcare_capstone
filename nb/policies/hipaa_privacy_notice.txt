TITLE: How Your Health Information Is Used and Protected
CATEGORY: Privacy & Compliance
AUDIENCE: Patients, all platform users

WHAT INFORMATION THIS PLATFORM HOLDS
Referral details, uploaded documents (referral letters, imaging/lab reports), appointment records,
medical records, and insurance details you or your care team provide, tied to your account.

WHO CAN SEE IT
Access follows a minimum-necessary principle, enforced by the platform, not just described here:
- You can always see your own referrals, appointments, medical records, and insurance on file.
- Your referring doctor and any specialist you're actually referred to can see records tied to
  that specific referral or appointment — not your entire history with other providers.
- Care coordination staff can see referral status platform-wide, to help move referrals along, but
  clinical records (appointments, medical records) stay limited to staff who are actually a party
  to them.
- Payer-side staff can see referral and analytics data for oversight, but never clinical
  appointment or medical-record detail.
- Every access to your data — by a person or by the AI assistant on your behalf — is scoped to
  what that specific role is allowed to see, checked on every single request rather than assumed
  from a login screen once.

CONSENT
Sharing your information across a boundary — for example, a specialist outside your immediate care
team, or a payer — requires an active consent on file before it happens. No cross-boundary share
happens silently.

HOW THE AI ASSISTANT USES YOUR DATA
The conversational assistant only ever answers using data the same access rules above already
allow the person asking to see. A patient's assistant session can only ever discuss that patient's
own records — it is not able to look up another patient's information, by ID or otherwise, and
will say so directly if asked.

AUDIT TRAIL
Every action that creates, changes, or reveals a record — including the AI assistant's own tool
calls — is logged with who did it, what they did, and when. This log exists to make access
reviewable, not to track you; it is visible to platform administrators for oversight, not to every
user.

RECORD RETENTION
Records are never silently deleted. Removing a record marks it as removed rather than erasing it,
preserving the audit trail, and it can be restored by an administrator if removed in error.
