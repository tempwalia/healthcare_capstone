TITLE: How Specialist Appointments Get Approved and Booked
CATEGORY: Scheduling & Appointments
AUDIENCE: Patients, care coordinators, specialists

SPECIALIST APPROVAL
A specialist recommendation is never auto-booked. When a referral reaches the "Selecting a
specialist" stage, the platform presents a ranked list of candidates, each with a stated reason
(in-network status, distance, rating, next available slot). A care coordinator or the assigned
specialist must explicitly confirm a candidate before booking proceeds. This is a deliberate
human-in-the-loop control, not a missing feature — a specialist match that looks correct on paper
(insurance, distance, rating) can still be wrong for reasons the platform can't see (a scheduling
conflict, a patient preference expressed outside the system, a clinical nuance), so a person
always has the final say.

If the referring doctor or patient already has a specific specialist in mind, that preference can
be recorded on the referral up front — the platform skips the recommendation step entirely and
goes straight to booking with that specialist once eligibility is confirmed.

APPOINTMENT BOOKING
Once a specialist is confirmed, the platform books the next available slot with that doctor
automatically, based on the doctor's posted availability. Appointments can also be booked directly
by a patient without going through a referral at all, for a doctor's open self-service slots.

WAIT-TIME TARGETS
Every referral has a target wait time in days (14 days if none is specified). If no slot is
available with the confirmed specialist inside that window, the referral is flagged for a care
coordinator to review — the usual next step is considering an alternative specialist or a
different timeframe, not leaving the patient waiting indefinitely.

RESCHEDULING AND CANCELLATION
An appointment can be cancelled by the patient, the assigned doctor, or care coordination staff.
Cancelling does not delete the referral itself — a cancelled appointment can be rebooked without
starting the referral process over from the beginning.

AFTER THE APPOINTMENT
Once the appointment has happened, the treating doctor (or a care coordinator relaying the visit
on the doctor's behalf) records the consult outcome directly on the appointment or referral. This
closes the loop: the patient, referring doctor, and care team can all see the recorded outcome and
the generated care-journey summary afterward.
